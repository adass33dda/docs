Get Sanity into your server and get the essentials configured before you close this tab. Everything below assumes the default `,` prefix.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite Sanity">
    Head to [sanity.ws](https://sanity.ws) and click **Invite**. Pick your server and keep the default permissions. Sanity needs them to moderate, ban, and manage channels.

    <Warning>
      Do not strip Sanity's role of permissions after inviting. If it can't ban or manage roles, antinuke and antiraid can't defend your server.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the setup command">
    In any channel Sanity can see, run:

    ```txt Setup
    ,setup
    ```

    This creates the jail role, all three mute roles (mute, image mute, reaction mute), a `#jail` channel, and a `#case-log` channel. One command and moderation is ready to go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on antinuke">
    Antinuke is the single most important thing you'll enable. It watches for mass bans, mass channel deletes, and role wipes, then punishes whoever did it.

    ```txt Enable antinuke
    ,antinuke enable
    ```

    Then whitelist yourself and anyone you fully trust:

    ```txt Whitelist an admin
    ,antinuke admin @yourname
    ```

    Full setup lives on the [Antinuke page](/security/antinuke).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on antiraid">
    Antiraid catches raid waves before they land. Enable mass-join protection with a sane default:

    ```txt Mass join
    ,antiraid massjoin on --threshold 5 --do ban --lock true --punish true
    ```

    That bans anyone joining faster than 5 accounts in a short window and locks the server for the duration. Tune the numbers to your join rate on the [Antiraid page](/security/antiraid).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give your staff fake permissions">
    Don't hand out real Discord permissions to moderators. Give them fake ones instead so a compromised staff account can't nuke you.

    ```txt Grant a moderator role
    ,fakepermissions grant @moderator manage_messages moderate_members kick_members
    ```

    Recommended role tiers are documented on the [Fake Permissions page](/security/fakepermissions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're done">
    Your server is now protected against nukes, raids, and rogue staff. Head to the [Security overview](/security/overview) to fine-tune everything or the [FAQ](/faq) if something isn't working.
  </Step>
</Steps>
