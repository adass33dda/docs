Sanity's security features stack. Antinuke stops rogue admins, antiraid stops raid waves, fake permissions stop compromised staff, and moderation handles day-to-day incidents. This page helps you pick the right combination for your server.

## Recommended setups

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Small (under 500)">
    You mostly need moderation and a light safety net. Raiders rarely target small servers, but one bad admin can still ruin your day.

    - Run `,setup` to provision moderation.
    - Enable `,antinuke enable` and whitelist yourself only.
    - Skip antiraid unless you've been hit before.
    - Give one trusted person `,fakepermissions grant` moderator perms.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mid-size (500 to 5k)">
    You're big enough to be noticed. Turn on everything.

    - Full moderation via `,setup`.
    - Antinuke enabled with a tight admin whitelist (owner plus 1 to 2 co-owners).
    - Antiraid mass-join at threshold 5, action ban, lock true.
    - Fake permissions for all staff tiers. See the tiers on the [Fake Permissions page](/security/fakepermissions).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Large (5k+)">
    Assume you'll be attacked. Configure aggressively and audit regularly.

    - All of the above.
    - Antiraid mass-join at threshold 3, plus new-account filtering.
    - Antinuke thresholds tuned down (fewer allowed actions before punish).
    - No real Discord admin permissions handed to anyone except the owner.
    - Review `,antinuke admin list` and `,fakepermissions list` monthly.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How the features fit together

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Antinuke" icon="fingerprint" href="/security/antinuke">
    Stops trusted admins (or their hijacked accounts) from mass banning, wiping channels, or deleting roles.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Antiraid" icon="person-to-door" href="/security/antiraid">
    Stops external attackers: mass joins, new-account waves, and spam DMs.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Fake permissions" icon="mask" href="/security/fakepermissions">
    Lets moderators do their job through Sanity without holding real Discord permissions.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Moderation" icon="gavel" href="/security/moderation">
    Day-to-day: warns, mutes, jails, kicks, and bans with a full case log.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The rule of least permission

<Tip>
  The single best thing you can do for your server's security: no one but you should have real Discord admin. Everyone else moderates through Sanity via fake permissions. If a staff account gets compromised, the attacker has nothing.
</Tip>

## After an incident

If someone got past your defenses, don't panic. Sanity keeps a full audit trail.

- Check `#case-log` for what happened and who did it.
- Run `,antinuke admin list` to see who was whitelisted at the time.
- Revoke anyone who shouldn't be there with `,antinuke admin remove @user`.
- Head to the [FAQ](/faq) for recovery steps.
